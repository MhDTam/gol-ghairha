# Call My Bluff · گول غيرها

**Iraqi cards. A game of bluff.**

[**▶ Play now**](https://gol-ghairha.onrender.com) · [العربية](README.ar.md)

A four-player card game of bold claims and well-timed challenges, set around an emerald-and-gold table with Iraqi-inspired card art. Play with friends online or practice against three bots, directly in your browser.

[![Call My Bluff — gameplay in English](media/gameplay-en.png)](https://gol-ghairha.onrender.com)

## The game

- **Play your way:** create a room, share its invitation, or join with a room code.
- **Practice solo:** take a seat against three bots, with no account required.
- **Arabic & English:** switch languages from the in-game language button.
- **Desktop & mobile:** a responsive table with animated cards and sound effects.

## How to play

1. Open [the game](https://gol-ghairha.onrender.com), choose a nickname, and start a room or practice match.
2. Play 1–4 cards face down and claim they match the required rank, progressing from ace through king.
3. Challenge a suspicious claim. A caught bluffer takes the pile; an incorrect challenger takes it instead. Empty your hand and survive the final challenge to win.
